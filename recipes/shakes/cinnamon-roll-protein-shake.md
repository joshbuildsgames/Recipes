# Cinnamon Roll Protein Shake

Phase 2, shake.

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops Thorne MediClear Plus
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
- ½ cup of frozen cauliflower
- 1 tsp of cinnamon
- 1 tsp of Vermont maple syrup
- Ice (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth. Blend extra to ensure the cauliflower is
   completely broken down.

## Notes

- This shake delivers warm, comforting flavor without spiking blood sugar. Cauliflower
  adds creaminess, while cinnamon supports glucose control. Ideal for post-workout
  recovery or as a satisfying meal replacement when you want something cozy but balanced.
