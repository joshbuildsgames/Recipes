# Vanilla Performance Protein Shake

Phase 2, shake.

## Ingredients

- 1 scoop Thorne Vanilla whey protein
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 banana
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- Ice (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.

## Notes

- This shake delivers balanced energy with a full banana for natural carbohydrates,
  protein for muscle support, and healthy fats to slow digestion and promote satiety.
  Ideal for post-workout or as a balanced meal replacement.
