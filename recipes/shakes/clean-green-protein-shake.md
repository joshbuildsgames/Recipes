# Clean Green Protein Shake

Phase 1, shake.

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops Thorne MediClear Plus **or** 2 scoops Thorne Vegan Protein
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
- Handful baby spinach, about ½ cup
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Ice (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.

## Notes

- This shake is nutrient-rich and easy on digestion.
- Whey protein is not approved in Phase 1.
- Make sure your almond milk is unsweetened.
