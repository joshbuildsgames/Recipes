# Chocolate Banana Protein Shake

Phase 2, shake.

## Ingredients

- 1 scoop Thorne Chocolate whey protein
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 banana
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- Ice (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.

## Notes

- Chia seeds provide fiber and healthy fats to slow digestion and help maintain stable
  blood sugar, while chocolate whey adds a satisfying flavor without excess sugar.
