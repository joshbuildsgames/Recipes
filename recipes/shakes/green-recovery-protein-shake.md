# Green Recovery Protein Shake

Phase 2, shake.

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops Thorne MediClear Plus
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
- ½ avocado
- Handful of spinach
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Ice (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.

## Notes

- This shake is designed to support recovery, digestion, and inflammation control.
  Healthy fats from avocado slow digestion, while greens and lemon support detox pathways
  and nutrient absorption. Ideal post-workout or as a light, nutrient-dense meal
  replacement.
