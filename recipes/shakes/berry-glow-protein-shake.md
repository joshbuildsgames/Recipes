# Berry Glow Protein Shake

Phase 2, shake.

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops Thorne MediClear Plus
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
- ½ cup frozen blueberries
- ½ cup frozen strawberries
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- Ice (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.

## Notes

- Two full scoops of MediClear provide a complete protein base, while berries
  reintroduce natural sugars and antioxidants. Almond butter adds satiety and helps slow
  the glucose response, making this shake balanced enough for either post-workout
  recovery or a meal replacement.
