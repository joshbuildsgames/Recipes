# Smoked Salmon Breakfast Plate

Phase 1, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 4 oz smoked salmon
- 3 large eggs, hard-boiled
- 2 cups arugula
- Lemon juice

## Method

1. Slice hard-boiled eggs.
2. Plate salmon, eggs, and arugula.
3. Finish with lemon juice and serve.
