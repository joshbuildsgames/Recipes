# Savory Breakfast Bowl

Phase 1, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- ½ cup roasted sweet potato cubes
- 2 cups arugula
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Spray skillet and scramble eggs.
2. Add sweet potato to reheat.
3. Add eggs and sweet potato to bowl. Toss and serve.

## Notes

- For additional protein, use liquid egg whites to increase protein without increasing fat.
  - ¼ cup = 6–7g protein
  - ½ cup = 13–14g protein
