# Greek Yogurt & Raspberry Bowl

Phase 2, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup of Fage 0% Greek yogurt
- ½ cup raspberries
- ¼ cup pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
- ½ tbsp honey

## Method

1. Add Greek yogurt to a bowl.
2. Top with raspberries and pumpkin seeds.
3. Drizzle with honey.
4. Stir gently and enjoy.

## Notes

- This bowl provides a balanced mix of protein, fiber, and healthy fats. Pumpkin seeds
  add magnesium and zinc for recovery, while raspberries and honey supply
  quick-digesting carbs to support energy and performance. Best used as a Phase 2
  breakfast or post-workout snack.
