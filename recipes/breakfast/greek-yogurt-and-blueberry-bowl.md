# Greek Yogurt & Blueberry Bowl

Phase 2, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup of Fage 0% Greek yogurt
- ½ cup of blueberries
- 1 tbsp of almond butter

## Method

1. Add Greek yogurt to a bowl.
2. Top with blueberries and almond butter.
3. Stir gently and enjoy.

## Notes

- This bowl provides a clean reintroduction of dairy with high-quality protein and
  gut-friendly probiotics. Blueberries add natural carbohydrates and antioxidants, while
  almond butter supports satiety and blood sugar balance. Ideal for a light breakfast or
  post-workout option during Phase 2.
