# Fluffy Greek Yogurt Pancakes

Phase 2, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Fage 0% Greek yogurt
- 2 large eggs
- ½ cup gluten-free rolled oats (blended into flour)
- 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of sea salt
- 2–4 tbsp unsweetened almond milk (to thin batter as needed)
- Avocado oil spray (for cooking)

## Method

1. Blend the rolled oats in a blender until a fine flour forms.
2. In a bowl, whisk together Greek yogurt, eggs, vanilla, and maple syrup.
3. Add oat flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt. Mix until smooth.
4. Add almond milk 1 tablespoon at a time until batter is thick but pourable.
5. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat and lightly coat with avocado oil spray.
6. Spoon batter onto the pan and gently spread into pancakes.
7. Cook 2–3 minutes until bubbles form, flip, and cook another 1–2 minutes.
8. Repeat with remaining batter.

## Notes

- **This is not a complete meal.** Pair 2 pancakes with 2 eggs and a ½ cup of berries.
- Recipe makes 4 servings.
- These pancakes use gluten-free oats and Greek yogurt, making them appropriate for
  Phase 2 reintroduction. The combination of protein, fiber, and slow-digesting
  carbohydrates supports blood sugar balance and energy. Maple syrup provides a small
  amount of natural sweetness without refined sugars.
