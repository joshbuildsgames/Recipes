# Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash

Phase 1, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- ½ cup cooked diced sweet potato
- Avocado oil spray (light spray to coat pan)
- Sea salt & paprika

## Method

1. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium heat.
2. Add sweet potato; cook until heated through.
3. Add eggs and scramble or cook to preference. Season and serve.

## Notes

- For additional protein, use liquid egg whites to increase protein without increasing fat.
  - ¼ cup = 6–7g protein
  - ½ cup = 13–14g protein
