# Saladino Mudbowls

Phase 2, breakfast.

## Ingredients

**Oats (makes 4 servings)**
- 1¼ cups of gluten-free steel-cut oats
- 4 cups of water

**Per serving**
- ½ cup frozen blueberries
- 2 scoops of Thorne Chocolate Whey
- 1 tbsp of ground flax seed

## Method

**Step 1: Cook the oats**

1. Add oats and water to a slow cooker.
2. Cook on low for 8 hours. Oats can remain on the warm setting if needed.

**Step 2: Build your bowl**

1. Scoop 1 cup cooked oats into a bowl.
2. Add frozen berries, whey protein, and ground flaxseed.
3. Mix well until fully combined and creamy.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 4 servings.
- This bowl delivers a balanced combination of complex carbs, fiber, and protein to
  support energy, satiety, and recovery. It's ideal as a high-performance breakfast or
  post-training meal.
