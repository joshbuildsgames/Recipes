# Red Onion & Pepper Egg Scramble

Phase 2, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- ½ cup diced red bell pepper
- ¼ cup diced red onion
- Avocado oil spray (light spray to coat pan)
- Sea salt & black pepper

## Method

1. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium heat.
2. Add red onion and bell pepper; sauté 2–3 minutes until softened.
3. Add eggs and scramble gently until just set.
4. Season with sea salt and black pepper. Serve warm.

## Notes

- For additional protein, use liquid egg whites to increase protein without increasing fat.
  - ¼ cup = 6–7g protein
  - ½ cup = 13–14g protein
