# Veggie Power Scramble

Phase 1, breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- ½ cup sliced mushrooms
- ¼ cup diced onion
- Avocado oil spray (light spray to coat pan)
- Sea salt & black pepper

## Method

1. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium heat.
2. Sauté onion and mushrooms until softened.
3. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
4. Scramble in eggs and cook until set.

## Notes

- For additional protein, use liquid egg whites to increase protein without increasing fat.
  - ¼ cup = 6–7g protein
  - ½ cup = 13–14g protein
