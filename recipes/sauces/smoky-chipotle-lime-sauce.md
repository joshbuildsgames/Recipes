# Smoky Chipotle Lime Sauce

One of three sauces for dressing up plain chicken. The heat is adjustable — chipotle in
adobo varies a lot by brand.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup light mayonnaise **or** Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon chipotle in adobo sauce (or more to taste)
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon honey
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

1. Whisk everything together until smooth. Mince the chipotle first, or blend it if you
   want the sauce fully smooth rather than flecked.
2. Taste and add more chipotle for heat.
3. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Makes about 1–2 servings. Double or quadruple to scale.
- The original post listed ingredients only; the method above is the obvious reading.
- The yogurt version is the leaner, higher-protein swap; the mayo version is richer and
  clings better to hot chicken.
- Leftover chipotles in adobo freeze well — spoon them onto parchment in single-pepper
  portions and bag them once solid.
