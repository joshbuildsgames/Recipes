# Creamy Avocado Lime Sauce

One of three sauces for dressing up plain chicken.

## Ingredients

- 1 ripe avocado
- ¼ cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Water to adjust consistency

## Method

1. Blend the avocado, yogurt, lime juice, and garlic until smooth.
2. Add water a tablespoon at a time until it pours the way you want it.
3. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Makes about 1–2 servings. Double or quadruple to scale.
- The original post listed ingredients only; the method above is the obvious reading.
- Avocado browns once cut, so this is best made close to serving. The lime juice slows
  it but does not stop it.
