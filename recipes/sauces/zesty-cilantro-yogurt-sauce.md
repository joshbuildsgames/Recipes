# Zesty Cilantro Yogurt Sauce

One of three sauces for dressing up plain chicken. The leanest of the three, and the
highest in protein.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

1. Stir everything together, or blend for a smoother, greener sauce.
2. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Makes about 1–2 servings. Double or quadruple to scale.
- The original post listed ingredients only; the method above is the obvious reading.
- Toasting the cumin in a dry pan for 30 seconds before adding it deepens the flavor
  noticeably.
