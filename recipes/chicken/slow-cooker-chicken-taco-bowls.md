# Slow Cooker Chicken Taco Bowls

Phase 2, slow cooker or Dutch oven recipe.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced fire roasted tomatoes with green chilies
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp sea salt

## Method

1. Add all ingredients to the slow cooker.
2. Cook on LOW for 6–8 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours.
3. Shred chicken directly in the crockpot and stir to combine.
4. Split into 4 equal servings.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 4 servings.
- This recipe is Phase 2–appropriate as it reintroduces legumes and nightshades in a
  controlled, balanced way. Each serving provides approximately 6 oz of cooked chicken
  and is naturally high in protein and fiber, making it a satisfying and supportive meal
  for digestion and blood sugar balance.
