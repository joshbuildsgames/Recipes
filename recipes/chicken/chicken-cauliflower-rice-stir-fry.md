# Chicken Cauliflower Rice Stir-Fry

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 6 oz cooked chicken breast
- 1½ cups cauliflower rice
- ½ cup diced zucchini
- Avocado oil spray
- Sea salt & black pepper

## Method

1. Lightly spray a nonstick skillet with avocado oil spray and heat over medium.
2. Add diced chicken breast and sauté until heated through and lightly browned.
3. Add cauliflower rice and zucchini; cook 3–5 minutes until tender but not mushy.
4. Season with sea salt and black pepper. Serve hot.
