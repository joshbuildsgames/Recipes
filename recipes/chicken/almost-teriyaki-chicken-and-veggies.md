# Almost Teriyaki Chicken & Veggies

Phase 2, slow cooker or Dutch oven recipe.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- ½ cup coconut aminos
- 1 tbsp raw honey
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar or rice vinegar
- 3 cups broccoli florets

**Optional**
- ½ cup of cooked jasmine rice with each serving

## Method

1. Add chicken, coconut aminos, honey, garlic powder, ginger, and vinegar to the slow
   cooker.
2. Stir to evenly coat the chicken.
3. Cook on LOW for 6–8 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours.
4. Once cooked, shred chicken with forks.
5. Add broccoli during the last 30 minutes of cooking and stir gently.
6. Split into 6 equal servings.
7. **Toppers:** ½ tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp coconut aminos, a sprinkle of toasted sesame
   seeds, and/or a few chopped scallions can be added just before serving for extra
   flavor and a more traditional teriyaki finish. All of which are Phase 2 compliant.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 6 servings.
- **Updated macros with ½ cup jasmine rice per serving:** ~375 calories, ~28g carbohydrates.
- This version replaces traditional teriyaki sauce with coconut aminos, keeping the dish
  soy-free and lower in sugar. A small amount of honey balances the acidity and mimics
  classic teriyaki flavor without spiking blood sugar.
