# Almost Steak Fried Rice

Phase 2 recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs top sirloin steak, cut into small cubes
- 2 cups cooked jasmine rice (about ⅔ cup dry)
- 3 large eggs, whisked
- 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, green beans — no corn)
- 1 tbsp avocado oil or olive oil
- 1–2 tbsp coconut aminos (soy-free)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- Sea salt & black pepper, to taste
- Optional garnish: sliced green onions or fresh cilantro

## Method

1. Cook the rice according to package directions and set aside.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add oil.
3. Add steak cubes, season with salt and pepper, and sear until cooked to desired
   doneness. Remove from pan and set aside.
4. In the same pan, add whisked eggs and scramble until just set.
5. Push eggs to one side and add frozen vegetables. Cook until heated through.
6. Add cooked rice and return steak to the pan.
7. Season with coconut aminos, garlic powder, ginger, and additional salt if needed.
8. Stir well and cook for 1–2 minutes until everything is evenly combined and heated
   through.
9. Split into 4 equal servings.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 4 servings.
- The combination of lean steak, eggs, and rice supports energy and recovery, while
  cooking methods stay gentle on digestion. It's easy to prep, easy to digest, and ideal
  for consistent Phase 2 meals.
