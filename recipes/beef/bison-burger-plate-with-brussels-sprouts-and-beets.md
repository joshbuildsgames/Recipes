# Bison Burger Plate with Brussels Sprouts & Beets

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 5 oz bison patty
- 1 cup Brussels sprouts
- ¾ cup roasted beets
- 1 tsp avocado oil
- Salt, pepper

## Method

1. Toss Brussels sprouts and beets with avocado oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at
   **400°F** until tender.
2. Grill or pan-cook bison patty until cooked through.
3. Serve plated, bun-free.
