# Steak & Spinach Bowl

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 5 oz cooked sirloin steak
- 2 cups spinach
- ½ cup roasted sweet potato
- Avocado oil spray
- 1 tsp olive oil

## Method

1. Cook steak to desired doneness.
2. Lightly wilt spinach.
3. Combine steak, spinach, and sweet potato in a bowl.
4. Drizzle with olive oil and serve.
