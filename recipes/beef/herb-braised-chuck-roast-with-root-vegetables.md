# Herb-Braised Chuck Roast with Root Vegetables

Phase 2, slow cooker or Dutch oven recipe.

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs beef chuck roast, cut into large chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups carrots, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
- 3 cups parsnips, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
- 3 cups sweet potatoes (Japanese or regular), peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
- 2 cups beef bone broth (ensure clean ingredients, no additives)
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar or balsamic vinegar
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 1 tsp sea salt (or to taste)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder

## Method

1. Pat beef chunks dry and season evenly with sea salt, garlic powder, and onion powder.
   Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear beef on all sides until
   deeply browned. Remove and set aside.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion and garlic to the pot and sauté for 3–4 minutes until
   softened and fragrant.
3. Pour in beef broth and vinegar, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot
   with a wooden spoon.
4. Return beef to the pot. Add bay leaves, thyme, rosemary, carrots, parsnips, and sweet
   potatoes. Ensure vegetables are mostly submerged in liquid.
5. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for **2½–3 hours**,
   or until meat is fork-tender and vegetables are soft.
   *Alternatively:* transfer the covered pot to a **325°F** oven for the same cooking time.
6. Remove and discard bay leaves and herb stems. Shred beef slightly with forks. Split
   into 8 servings.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 8 servings.
- This recipe uses minimally processed ingredients and slow cooking to support digestion.
  It's a hearty, anti-inflammatory meal that's ideal for batch cooking and fully
  Phase 2–compliant.
