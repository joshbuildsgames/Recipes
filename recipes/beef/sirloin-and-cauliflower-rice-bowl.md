# Sirloin & Cauliflower Rice Bowl

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 5 oz cooked sirloin steak
- 1 cup cauliflower rice
- 1 cup chopped asparagus
- Avocado oil spray
- Sea salt & black pepper

## Method

1. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil spray and heat over medium.
2. Sauté cauliflower rice and asparagus for 3–4 minutes until tender.
3. Add sliced sirloin and cook just until heated through.
4. Season with sea salt and black pepper. Serve immediately.
