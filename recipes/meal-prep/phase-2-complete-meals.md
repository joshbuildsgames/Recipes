# Phase 2 — 6 Complete Meals

Balanced and repeatable. Each meal is assembled from the Phase 2 bulk meal prep bases:
two proteins, two vegetables, and two carbs. Every one follows the same method —
**reheat the components in a skillet or microwave until warm, then plate and serve.**

Macros are per serving.

| # | Protein | Vegetable | Carb | Cal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 oz garlic paprika chicken | 1 cup maple Brussels sprouts | ½ cup Yukon gold potatoes | 520 | 38g | 38g | 12g |
| 2 | 5 oz garlic paprika chicken | 1 cup red pepper & zucchini | ½ cup roasted red potatoes | 500 | 36g | 36g | 11g |
| 3 | 5 oz garlic paprika chicken | 1 cup maple Brussels sprouts | ½ cup roasted red potatoes | 500 | 37g | 37g | 12g |
| 4 | 5 oz maple meatballs | 1 cup red pepper & zucchini | ½ cup roasted red potatoes | 530 | 31g | 38g | 15g |
| 5 | 5 oz maple meatballs | 1 cup red pepper & zucchini | ½ cup Yukon gold potatoes | ~540 | 32g | 39g | 15g |
| 6 | 5 oz maple meatballs | 1 cup maple Brussels sprouts | ½ cup Yukon gold potatoes | ~530 | 32g | 38g | 15g |

## Notes

- **Meals 1–3** — balanced, comforting, and ideal post-workout. A good introduction to
  nightshades.
- **Meals 5–6** — higher satiety meals; ideal for evenings or high-output days.
- Dozens of further combinations are possible by mixing either protein base with either
  vegetable and either carb.
