# Roasted Carrot & Broccoli (Veggie #2)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Veggie #2.


## Ingredients

- 2 cups thinly sliced carrots
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- Avocado oil spray
- Optional: pinch of ground ginger to support digestion

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. Spray carrots and broccoli with avocado oil and season until evenly coated.
3. Spread in a single layer on a lined baking sheet.
4. Roast for 22–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until carrots are tender and
   broccoli is lightly crisped on the edges.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Hearty and great for batch cooking.
- Slice the carrots thinly so they cook evenly with the broccoli florets.
