# Ground Beef (Protein Base #2)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Protein base #2.


## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (90–93% lean)
- ½ cup finely diced yellow onion
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion and sauté for 2–3 minutes until soft and translucent.
3. Add ground beef.
4. Season with spices.
5. Cook until browned and fully cooked through, about 7–9 minutes.
6. Drain excess fat if needed, depending on the leanness of the beef.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Macros vary slightly based on beef leanness.
- Savory flavor profile.
