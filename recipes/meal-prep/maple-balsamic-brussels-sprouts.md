# Maple-Balsamic Brussels Sprouts (Veggie #1)

Phase 2 bulk meal prep. Veggie #1.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb Brussels sprouts, halved
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp sea salt
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. Drizzle with balsamic vinegar and maple syrup, then sprinkle with sea salt.
3. Toss gently to coat, then spread evenly in a single layer.
4. Roast for 25–30 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until tender and caramelized
   on the edges.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
