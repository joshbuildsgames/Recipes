# Ground Turkey (Protein Base #1)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Protein base #1.


## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey (90–93% lean)
- ½ cup finely diced yellow onion
- 1–2 tsp avocado oil
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion and sauté for 2–3 minutes until soft and translucent.
3. Add ground turkey.
4. Season with spices.
5. Cook until turkey is fully cooked through and no pink remains (about 6–8 minutes).

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Macros vary slightly based on turkey leanness.
- Light and clean flavor profile.
