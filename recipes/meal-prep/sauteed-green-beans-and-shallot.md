# Sautéed Green Beans & Shallot (Veggie #3)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Veggie #3.


## Ingredients

- 4 cups fresh green beans, trimmed
- 1 shallot, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and lightly coat with avocado oil.
2. Add green beans and sauté for 4–5 minutes until bright green and just tender.
3. Add sliced shallot and garlic powder.
4. Cook another 2–3 minutes until softened and fragrant.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Crisp, light, and very easy on digestion.
