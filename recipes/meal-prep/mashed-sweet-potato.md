# Mashed Sweet Potato (Carb #2)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Carb #2.


## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1–2 tsp avocado oil
- Sea salt to taste

## Method

1. Boil sweet potatoes in salted water until fork-tender (15–20 minutes).
2. Drain and mash with avocado oil and salt until smooth.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Comforting and easy on digestion.
