# Roasted Red Potatoes with Rosemary (Carb #2)

Phase 2 bulk meal prep. Carb #2.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb red potatoes, quartered
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp fresh or dried rosemary
- 1 tsp sea salt

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. Toss potatoes with oil, rosemary, and salt.
3. Roast 30 minutes, stirring once, until crisp outside and tender inside.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Red potatoes are hearty and hold their shape well when cooked, making them ideal for
  roasting, boiling, or smashing. They pair especially well with lean proteins and help
  create satisfying, balanced meals. As a nightshade vegetable, they are best introduced
  in Phase 2 once digestion has stabilized.
