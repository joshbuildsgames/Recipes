# Roasted Sweet Potato Cubes (Carb #1)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Carb #1.


## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- ½ tsp sea salt
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. Toss sweet potatoes with oil and salt.
3. Spread on a lined baking sheet.
4. Roast 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly browned.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Classic and simple flavors. Easy to digest.
