# Roasted Yukon Gold Potatoes (Carb #1)

Phase 2 bulk meal prep. Carb #1.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb Yukon gold potatoes, cubed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. Toss potatoes with oil, salt, and garlic powder.
3. Spread in a single layer on a baking sheet.
4. Roast for 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and tender.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Creamy texture makes these easy to digest. Great post-workout carb source.
