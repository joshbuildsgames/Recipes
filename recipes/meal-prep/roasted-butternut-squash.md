# Roasted Butternut Squash (Carb #4)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Carb #4.


## Ingredients

- 3 cups cubed butternut squash
- ½ tsp sea salt
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. Toss squash with oil and salt.
3. Roast 25–30 minutes until soft and caramelized.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Slightly sweet and very filling.
