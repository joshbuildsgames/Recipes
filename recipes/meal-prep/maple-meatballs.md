# Maple Meatballs (Protein Base #2)

Phase 2 bulk meal prep. Protein base #2.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb lean ground beef (90–93%)
- 1 large egg
- 3 tbsp Vermont maple syrup
- 2 tsp dried sage
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **400°F** and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a bowl, gently combine ground beef, egg, maple syrup, thyme, sage, and sea salt
   (do not overwork).
3. Form into 1½ inch meatballs and place evenly on the baking sheet.
4. Spray meatballs with avocado oil.
5. Bake for 20–25 minutes.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Cook to medium doneness to prevent drying out during reheat.
