# Zucchini Herb Sauté (Veggie #1)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Veggie #1.


## Ingredients

- 2 green zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 2 yellow zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and lightly coat with avocado oil.
2. Add zucchini and cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender but not mushy.
3. Season with spices.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Light and easy on digestion.
