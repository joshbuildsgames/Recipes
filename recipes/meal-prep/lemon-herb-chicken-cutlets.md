# Lemon Herb Chicken Cutlets (Protein Base #3)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Protein base #3.


## Ingredients

- 1½ lb thin-sliced chicken breast (about 8 cutlets)
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- Zest of ½ lemon
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Pat chicken dry with paper towels.
2. Season both sides with spices.
3. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium heat.
4. Cook chicken 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through
   (internal temp **165°F**).
5. Remove from pan and let rest 2–3 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Light and bright flavor profile.
