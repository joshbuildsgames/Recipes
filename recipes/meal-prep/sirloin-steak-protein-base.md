# Sirloin Steak (Protein Base #4)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Protein base #4.


## Ingredients

- 1½ lb top sirloin steak
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried rosemary
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Remove steak from refrigerator 15–20 minutes before cooking.
2. Pat dry thoroughly with paper towels.
3. Season both sides with spices.
4. Lightly spray a skillet with avocado oil and heat over medium-high heat.
5. Sear steak for 3–4 minutes per side (depending on thickness) until internal
   temperature reaches:
   - **130–135°F** for medium-rare
   - **135–140°F** for medium

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Let rest for 5–7 minutes before cutting.
- Slice against the grain for maximum tenderness.
