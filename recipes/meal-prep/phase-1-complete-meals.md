# Phase 1 — 12 Complete Meals

Balanced and repeatable. Each meal is assembled from the Phase 1 bulk meal prep bases:
four proteins, four vegetables, and four carbs. Every one follows the same method —
**reheat the components in a skillet or microwave until warm, then plate and serve.**

Macros are per serving.

| # | Protein | Vegetable | Carb | Cal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 oz lemon herb chicken | 1 cup zucchini herb sauté | ½ cup slow-roasted sweet potato | 415 | 36g | 30g | 7g |
| 2 | 5 oz lemon herb chicken | 1 cup green beans & shallots | ½ cup mashed sweet potato | 420 | 36g | 32g | 8g |
| 3 | 5 oz lemon herb chicken | 1 cup roasted cauliflower | ½ cup roasted butternut squash | 420 | 36g | 32g | 8g |
| 4 | 5 oz sirloin steak | 1 cup sautéed zucchini | ½ cup roasted sweet potato | 440 | 34g | 32g | 9g |
| 5 | 5 oz sirloin steak | 1 cup roasted carrots & broccoli | ½ cup mashed sweet potato | 450 | 34g | 34g | 9g |
| 6 | 5 oz sirloin steak | 1 cup green beans & shallots | ½ cup roasted butternut squash | 445 | 34g | 30g | 9g |
| 7 | 5 oz ground turkey | 1 cup zucchini herb sauté | ½ cup roasted sweet potato | 400 | 30g | 30g | 8g |
| 8 | 5 oz ground turkey | 1 cup roasted carrot & broccoli | ½ cup mashed sweet potato | 430 | 30g | 34g | 9g |
| 9 | 5 oz ground turkey | 1 cup green beans | ½ slow-roasted sweet potato, with the skin | 410 | 30g | 32g | 8g |
| 10 | 5 oz ground beef | 1 cup zucchini herb sauté | ½ cup roasted sweet potato | 440 | 32g | 32g | 11g |
| 11 | 5 oz ground beef | 1 cup roasted carrots & broccoli | ½ cup roasted butternut squash | 450 | 32g | 34g | 11g |
| 12 | 5 oz ground beef | 1 cup roasted cauliflower | ½ cup mashed sweet potato | 445 | 32g | 33g | 11g |

## Recommended Phase 1 approved toppers

- **Meal 1** — lemon zest or coconut aminos
- **Meal 2** — a pinch of sea salt on the green beans, a pinch of cinnamon on the sweet potatoes
- **Meal 3** — fresh thyme or parsley
- **Meal 4** — rosemary and a pinch of sea salt
- **Meal 5** — cinnamon on the sweet potatoes, a pinch of salt on the steak
- **Meal 6** — coconut aminos on the steak and green beans; fresh parsley or thyme on the butternut squash
- **Meal 7** — coconut aminos on the ground turkey, sea salt on the sweet potato
- **Meal 8** — coconut aminos on the ground turkey, cinnamon on the sweet potato
- **Meal 9** — coconut aminos on the ground turkey, cinnamon on the sweet potato
- **Meal 10** — coconut aminos on the ground beef, sea salt on the sweet potato
- **Meal 11** — fresh rosemary and sea salt
- **Meal 12** — coconut aminos on the ground beef, cinnamon on the sweet potato

## Notes

- Dozens of further combinations are possible by mixing any protein base with any
  vegetable and any carb.
