# Roasted Red Pepper & Zucchini Medley (Veggie #2)

Phase 2 bulk meal prep. Veggie #2.

## Ingredients

- 2 large red bell peppers, diced
- 2 large zucchini, half moons
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. Toss peppers and zucchini with oil, salt, paprika, and oregano.
3. Spread evenly on a baking sheet.
4. Roast for 22–25 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender and lightly caramelized.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Red bell peppers are one of the easiest nightshades to tolerate because they're
  naturally sweet and low in lectins when cooked.
