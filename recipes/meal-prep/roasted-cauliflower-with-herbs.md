# Roasted Cauliflower with Herbs (Veggie #4)

Phase 1 bulk meal prep. Veggie #4.


## Ingredients

- 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp dried rosemary or thyme
- Avocado oil spray
- Optional: pinch of ground turmeric for anti-inflammatory and digestion support

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. Toss cauliflower with oil and seasonings.
3. Spread evenly on a lined baking sheet.
4. Roast for 25–30 minutes, flipping once, until golden and tender.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Neutral and filling. Reheats well.
