# Garlic Paprika Chicken Breast (Protein Base #1)

Phase 2 bulk meal prep. Protein base #1.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **400°F**.
2. Pat chicken breasts dry with paper towels.
3. Season both sides with spices.
4. Lightly spray a baking dish with avocado oil and place chicken in a single layer.
5. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until internal temperature reaches **165°F**.
6. Remove from oven and let rest 5 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings.
- Baking keeps this protein lean, juicy, and easy for batch cooking.
