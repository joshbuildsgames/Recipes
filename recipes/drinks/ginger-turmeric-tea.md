# Ginger Turmeric Tea

Recipe makes 10 servings.

## Ingredients

- 10 cups of water
- ¼ cup of turmeric, sliced into rounds
- ¼ cup of ginger, sliced into rounds
- Optional: add a pinch of black pepper to your mug to help with curcumin absorption

## Method

1. Add water, ginger, and turmeric to a large pot.
2. Bring to a boil over high heat.
3. Reduce heat and simmer covered for 10–15 minutes, allowing the flavors and nutrients
   to infuse.
4. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
5. Store tea with ginger and turmeric slices in a glass jar.
6. Store in the refrigerator and reheat as needed throughout the week.

## Notes

- This tea is a gentle daily ritual that supports digestion, inflammation balance, and
  hydration. Fresh ginger helps stimulate digestion and reduce bloating, while turmeric
  supports the body's natural detoxification pathways and inflammatory response. The warm
  liquid soothes the gut lining and encourages regular digestion, making it especially
  helpful in colder months or during periods of digestive reset. This tea is caffeine-free,
  easy to tolerate, and ideal for daily use.
