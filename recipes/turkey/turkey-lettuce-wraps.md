# Turkey Lettuce Wraps

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 6 oz lean ground turkey
- ¼ cup diced onion
- ¼ cup shredded carrot
- 4 butter lettuce leaves
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. Spray skillet and cook turkey with onion.
2. Stir in carrots.
3. Spoon into lettuce leaves.
