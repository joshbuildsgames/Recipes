# Saladino Turkey Chili

Phase 2, slow cooker or Dutch oven recipe.

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs lean ground turkey
- 2 sweet Italian sausage links
- 1 can chili beans (15 oz)
- 2 bell peppers
- 1 zucchini
- 1 cup onions
- 2 cups of carrots
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 28 oz crushed tomatoes
- 1.5 cups turkey broth
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp of olive oil (halved)

## Method

1. Dice onions, peppers, zucchini, and carrots into uniform cubes for even cooking.
2. In a Dutch oven, heat ½ tbsp oil over medium heat.
3. Add onions and garlic.
4. Cook for ~10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until onions begin to brown.
5. Add peppers, zucchini, and carrots; cook for 10 minutes.
6. Once veggies are soft, add crushed tomatoes, rinsed beans, and broth.
7. In a separate skillet, heat the remaining ½ tbsp oil over medium heat.
8. Add ground meat. Cook until browned, breaking apart into crumbles. **Do NOT drain any
   liquid.**
9. Once meat is browned, stir in all dry spices. The spices will soak up any liquid in
   the pan.
10. Cook for 1–2 minutes to "bloom" the spices in the fat. This unlocks deeper flavor.
11. Add tomato paste. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
12. Add the meat mixture to the Dutch oven. Stir ingredients together and increase heat
    to bring to a soft boil.
13. As soon as you see bubbles, reduce heat.
14. In the meat skillet, add 2 sausage links. Cook until browned, breaking apart into
    crumbles. Add sausage to the Dutch oven.
15. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for at least 1 hour (or longer for richer
    flavor). Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 10 servings.
- This recipe traditionally includes Worcestershire sauce, but it has been removed to
  keep it compliant with the elimination diet.
