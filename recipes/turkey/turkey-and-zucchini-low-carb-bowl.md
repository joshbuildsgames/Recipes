# Turkey & Zucchini Low Carb Bowl

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 5 oz lean ground turkey
- 1 cup diced zucchini
- ¼ cup sliced avocado
- Avocado oil spray (light spray to coat pan)
- Sea salt & garlic powder

## Method

1. Lightly spray skillet and cook turkey until browned.
2. Add zucchini and cook until tender.
3. Top with avocado and serve.
