# Creamy Turkey & Spinach Soup

Phase 2, slow cooker or Dutch oven recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs lean ground turkey (93%)
- 1 can (13.5 oz) light coconut milk
- 1 carton (32 oz) low-sodium chicken broth
- 4 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup diced white potatoes (½-inch cubes)
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1 cup diced carrots
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp basil
- ½ tsp oregano
- ½ tsp thyme
- ½ tsp rosemary
- ½ tsp marjoram
- Sea salt & black pepper
- Avocado oil spray

## Method

1. In a large skillet, brown the ground turkey over medium heat until fully cooked.
   Drain excess liquid, if needed.
2. Transfer the cooked turkey to a slow cooker.
3. Add chicken broth, coconut milk, diced potatoes, onion, carrots, Italian seasoning,
   garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
4. Cook on LOW for 6–8 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours.
5. Add spinach during the final 30 minutes of cooking and stir until wilted.
6. Split into 6 equal servings.

## Notes

- Recipe makes 6 servings.
- White potatoes are a Phase 2 reintroduction that add gentle carbohydrates and natural
  thickness to the soup without overwhelming digestion. Coconut milk provides creaminess
  without dairy and should contain only coconut and water as ingredients. Together, these
  elements create a balanced, satisfying meal that supports recovery, steady energy, and
  fullness. This recipe is ideal for batch cooking and reheats well, making it an easy,
  nourishing option throughout the week.
