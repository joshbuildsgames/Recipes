# Wild Salmon with Spaghetti Squash & Asparagus

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 5 oz wild salmon
- 1½ cups cooked spaghetti squash
- 1 cup asparagus
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- Fresh herbs

## Method

1. Roast asparagus with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Bake or pan-sear salmon and season with herbs.
3. Warm spaghetti squash and season lightly.
4. Serve salmon over squash with asparagus.
