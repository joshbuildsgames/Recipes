# Salmon & Roasted Brussels Sprouts

Phase 1, additional meal idea.

## Ingredients

- 5 oz salmon fillet
- 1 cup Brussels sprouts
- Avocado oil spray
- Sea salt

## Method

1. Spray Brussels sprouts with oil and sprinkle with salt. Roast at **400°F** for
   20 minutes.
2. Bake or pan-sear salmon until flaky.
